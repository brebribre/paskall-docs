# Guides

| Guide | What it covers |
|---|---|

| [Remote Device Owner Setup](remote-device-owner-setup.md) | Granting Device Owner status to a screen that's already running, over Wi-Fi, with no USB cable and no factory reset. |
| [Instant Updates](instant-updates.md) | Why your screens find out about changes in moments instead of waiting up to 30 seconds, and what to expect while it rolls out. |
| [Building and Publishing the Player](building-and-publishing-the-player.md) | Building a new player release, uploading it to R2, and rolling it out to screens from the CMS. |
| [User Access Management](user-access-management.md) | Owners and managers, screen grants, what everyone shares, and the review step between a manager's save and a screen. |
